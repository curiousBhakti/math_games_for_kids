🧩 What are FACTORS?

Factors are the smaller numbers that multiply together to make a bigger number! They fit evenly inside with no leftovers.

Example for 12:
• 1 × 12 = 12
• 2 × 6 = 12
• 3 × 4 = 12
Factors of 12 = 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12

🚀 What are MULTIPLES?

Multiples are what you get when you multiply a number by 1, 2, 3, 4, etc. Think of skip-counting!

Example for 4:
• 4 × 1 = 4
• 4 × 2 = 8
• 4 × 3 = 12, 16, 20, 24...
Multiples of 4 = 4, 8, 12, 16, 20...
